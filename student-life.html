Student Life

A day at Macvak

Learning, play, worship and friendship, in a safe compound where every adult knows every child.

The school day

What a typical day looks like

TimeActivity
7:30 AMGates open. Children are received by staff and settle into their classrooms.
8:00 AMMorning assembly: devotion, the national anthem and pledge, notices and inspection.
8:30 AMMorning lessons in core subjects, when minds are freshest.
10:30 AMBreak and snack. Supervised play on the compound.
11:00 AMLessons continue: science, social studies, languages, creative arts, ICT.
12:30 PMLunch and rest time for the early years.
1:15 PMAfternoon lessons, reading, clubs, cadet drill or sport depending on the day.
3:00 PMClosing. JHS extra classes run after school on selected days.
A network of support

Every child known, every child cared for

Class teachers

Each class has a teacher responsible for the academic progress and wellbeing of every child in it.

Health and wellness

A first aid kit and trained staff on site, and prompt contact with parents whenever a child is unwell.

Safety and security

A walled compound, a single supervised gate and a sign-out system for pick-ups.

Faith and character

Daily devotion, Religious and Moral Education and a culture of respect for every person.

Discipline with love

Clear rules, consistent follow-through and a focus on helping children make better choices.

Parent communication

Termly reports, PTA meetings and a direct line to the head of school.

Uniform and expectations

Looking smart, being ready

Macvak students wear the school uniform with pride. Neat uniform, clean shoes and good grooming are part of the daily inspection at assembly. Physical education kit is worn on sports days and the Cadet Corps has its own drill uniform.

  • Punctuality: children should arrive by 7:45 AM
  • Respect for teachers, classmates and school property
  • Homework completed and books brought every day
  • Mobile phones are not permitted during the school day
