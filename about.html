Our School

Small enough to know every child. Serious enough to prepare them for life.

Macvak Kids Academy is a private day school in Afienya, in the Ningo Prampram district of the Greater Accra Region.

Mission

Our mission

To provide a safe, caring and disciplined learning environment where every child develops a love of learning, strong moral character and the academic skills to succeed in senior high school and beyond.

Our values
  • Excellence. We set high expectations and support every child to reach them.
  • Discipline. Good habits, respect and punctuality are taught and modelled every day.
  • Care. Children learn best when they feel safe, known and loved.
  • Faith and character. Honesty, kindness and service to others sit at the heart of school life.
  • Community. Parents, teachers and students working together.
Leadership

Meet our Head of School

Stephen Kumi

Stephen Kumi leads Macvak Kids Academy with a clear conviction: that a good basic education changes the direction of a child's life, and of their family's. Under his leadership the school has grown into a full Creche to JHS academy with a reputation for discipline, strong BECE preparation and a caring staff.

Mr. Kumi is personally involved in admissions and welcomes every prospective family to the campus. Book a visit to meet him.

Our teachers and staff

Our teaching staff are trained educators who follow the Ghana Education Service curriculum and take part in regular professional development. Class teachers at the Creche, Nursery and KG levels are supported by assistants so that our youngest learners get the attention they need.

Support staff keep the compound clean and secure, and every adult on campus is expected to model the values we teach.

Our campus

A bright, welcoming campus in Afienya

Macvak sits on the Appolonia to Afienya Road in the New Jerusalem area of Afienya. Our two-storey classroom block, painted in the school's signature colours, houses our Primary and JHS classes, while a separate block holds the school office and the early years classrooms.

  • Walled compound with a single supervised gate
  • Dedicated classrooms for every level from Creche to JHS 3
  • School office and staff room
  • Open compound for assembly, play, drill and sport
  • Close to Afienya, Dawhenya, Prampram and Appolonia

Governance

How the school is run

Proprietorship

Macvak Kids Academy is a privately owned school operating under the regulations of the Ghana Education Service and the Ningo Prampram District Education Directorate.

Parent partnership

We hold regular Parent Teacher Association meetings and share progress reports at the end of every term.

Safeguarding

All staff follow our child protection guidelines. Concerns can be raised directly with the head of school at any time.
