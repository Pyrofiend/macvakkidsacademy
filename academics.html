Academics

The academic experience

From first words in Creche to the BECE in JHS 3, our curriculum builds knowledge step by step and never loses sight of the individual child.

Our approach

Strong foundations, high expectations

We follow the Ghana Education Service Standards-Based Curriculum for KG and Primary and the Common Core Programme for Junior High School, enriched with daily reading, phonics and hands-on activities.

Small classes

Teachers know every learner's strengths and gaps and adjust lessons accordingly.

Regular assessment

Class tests, mid-term and end of term examinations, with reports shared with every parent.

Reading first

Phonics and daily reading from Nursery upward, because every other subject depends on it.

ICT and French

Computing skills and French are taught from the Primary level in line with the national curriculum.

Ghanaian language and culture

Children learn a Ghanaian language and take part in cultural dance and drumming.

Homework and support

Structured homework, extra classes for JHS and one-on-one support for children who need it.

Junior High School

Preparing for the BECE and senior high school

Our JHS programme is built around the Basic Education Certificate Examination. Students sit regular mock examinations, receive extra tuition in core subjects and get guidance on choosing senior high school programmes through the Computerised School Selection and Placement System.

  • Mock examinations in JHS 2 and JHS 3
  • Extra classes in English, Mathematics and Integrated Science
  • Guidance on senior high school selection
  • Study skills, time management and exam technique
