Find us

Directions

Macvak Kids Academy is in the New Jerusalem area of Afienya, on the Appolonia to Afienya Road, in the Ningo Prampram district of the Greater Accra Region. From the Accra to Aflao highway, turn off at Afienya towards Appolonia and the school is on the road, with its brightly painted classroom block clearly visible.

Macvak Kids Academy

A caring day school for children from Creche through Junior High School, serving families in Afienya, Prampram, Dawhenya, Appolonia and the wider Ningo Prampram district.
