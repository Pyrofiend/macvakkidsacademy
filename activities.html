Beyond the classroom

Cadets, sports and the arts

Character is built on the parade ground, on the pitch and on the stage as much as in the classroom.

Army Cadet Corps

Discipline, leadership and pride

The Macvak School Army Cadet Corps is one of the school's proudest traditions. Cadets train in drill, turnout and teamwork and represent the school at district and regional events.

The Corps took part in the Greater Accra Regional Cadet Drill Competition, where their calm and disciplined performance drew praise. Cadets also lead the school at Independence Day parades and on speech and prize-giving day.

  • Open to Upper Primary and JHS students
  • Weekly drill training on the school compound
  • District and regional drill competitions
  • Leadership ranks earned through commitment and conduct
Athletics

Sports at Macvak

Football

Boys' and girls' teams train on the compound and play friendly matches against neighbouring schools.

Athletics

Sprints, relays and field events, with an annual inter-house athletics competition.

Physical education

Weekly PE lessons for every class, from movement games in KG to team sports in JHS.

Inter-house competition

Students are grouped into houses that compete across the year in sport, quiz and drill.

Ampe, volleyball and games

Traditional and modern games at break time keep children active and happy.

Sportsmanship

We teach children to win humbly, lose gracefully and respect every opponent.

The arts

Music, drama and culture

Creative Arts is part of the national curriculum from KG upward, and at Macvak it comes alive through the school choir, drama productions, cultural dance and drumming, and visual art. Students perform at assemblies, PTA meetings and the end of year speech and prize-giving day.

  • School choir and singing at morning devotion
  • Cultural dance and drumming troupe
  • Drama and poetry recitals
  • Drawing, painting and craft displayed around the school
