Welcome

A school built on discipline, care and high expectations

Macvak Kids Academy serves families across Afienya, Prampram, Dawhenya and Appolonia. We believe every child can excel when they are known, loved and challenged.

From our brightly painted classroom blocks to our morning assemblies, everything at Macvak is designed to help children feel at home while they learn. Our teachers combine the national curriculum with hands-on activities, reading programmes and regular assessment so that no child is left behind.
