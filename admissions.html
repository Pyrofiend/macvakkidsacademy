Admissions

Join the Macvak family

We admit children into Creche, Nursery, Kindergarten, Primary and Junior High School throughout the year, subject to available places.

Enquire now
How to apply

Four simple steps

  1. Enquire

    Call +1 678 469 6185, email support@macvakkidsacademy.org or use the form below to tell us about your child.

  2. Visit the school

    Come to the campus on the Appolonia to Afienya Road, meet the head of school and see the classrooms. Visits are welcome on any school day.

  3. Complete the admission form

    Pick up an admission form at the school office and return it with the documents listed below.

  4. Placement and enrolment

    Children joining Primary or JHS take a short placement assessment so we can place them in the right class. Once fees are settled your child is enrolled and receives their uniform and book list.

Requirements

What to bring

  • Completed admission form
  • Child's birth certificate or weighing card (copy)
  • Two recent passport photographs of the child
  • Most recent report card or transfer letter, for Primary and JHS applicants
  • Immunisation record, for Creche, Nursery and KG applicants
  • Parent or guardian contact details and Ghana Card (copy)
Fees

Tuition and payment

Fees are set per level and per term and include tuition and continuous assessment. Uniforms, books, feeding and transport are billed separately where applicable. Payment plans are available on request.

Please contact the school office for the current fee schedule for your child's level.

Academic year

Our calendar

Macvak follows the Ghana Education Service academic calendar for basic schools, with three terms a year.

TermTypical periodHighlights
First termSeptember to DecemberNew admissions, Parent Teacher Association meeting, end of term examinations
Second termJanuary to AprilInter-class sports, Ghana's Independence Day parade with the Cadet Corps, mid-year reports
Third termMay to July or AugustBECE for JHS 3, speech and prize-giving day, graduation for KG 2 and JHS 3

Exact dates follow the GES calendar each year and are communicated to parents at the start of every term.
